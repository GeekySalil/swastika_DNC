BLUEPRINT & FEASIBILITY

Architectural Planning

From site studies to detailed floor plans and regulatory approvals — we handle both planning and technical drawings so every project starts with absolute clarity.

A timeless building requires a rational foundation. Our architectural planning process balances spatial functionality, passive solar orientation, natural ventilation, and Vastu harmony while strictly complying with local municipal zoning laws and Varanasi Development Authority (VDA) regulations.

Scope & Key Deliverables

  • Site analysis, orientation & zoning feasibility
  • Functional concept layouts & Vastu-aligned zoning
  • Detailed 2D construction working drawings
  • VDA & municipal approval documentation sets
  • Structural, MEP, and plumbing coordination

3D Visualization

TURNKEY EXECUTION

Complete Build & Construction

Full construction with material supply and management. One team handles contractors, materials, timelines, and budget from foundation to finishing.

By placing design and execution under one roof, we eliminate contractor-architect conflicts. Our civil engineering wing manages structural excavation, pile foundations, RCC frame casting, brick masonry, waterproofing, and high-spec architectural finishes. We control raw material quality and enforce milestone delivery.

Scope & Key Deliverables

  • End-to-end turnkey civil construction contracting
  • Lab-tested raw materials (TMT bars, cement, aggregates)
  • Structural RCC frame casting, shuttering & masonry
  • Integrated waterproofing, electrical & plumbing works
  • Strict budget accountability & milestone scheduling

05 / 05
QUALITY ASSURANCE & AUDIT

Site Supervision

Personal presence at every critical construction stage. We ensure what is drawn on paper is precisely what gets built — no shortcuts, no surprises.

Even the finest architectural drawing fails without on-site technical governance. Our principal designer and civil engineers personally inspect rebar layout, concrete pouring, curing cycles, plumb lines, and waterproofing seals. We hold contractors accountable to millimeter tolerances so the finished structure mirrors the original vision.

Scope & Key Deliverables

  • Routine site audits during crucial RCC & brickwork stages
  • Rebar tying, beam depth & shuttering verification
  • Concrete slump & compression testing supervision
  • Quality checklist sign-offs prior to slab casting
  • Direct client progress reports with photo logs
